# Roblox Hits (2025–2026) and the Production Process Behind Repeatable Hits

> **Method note for the report writer:** Researched 2026-10-22. In this session the network policy blocked direct page fetches, so I could not open devforum.roblox.com, Wikipedia, GamesBeat and similar sites. Every finding below comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, so treat exact quotes and numbers as "reported by the source", not checked against the full page. Player counts come from third-party trackers (Rolimons, RoStats, RoWatcher, RoMonitor), because Roblox does not publish live per-game CCU. Revenue figures are estimates unless noted otherwise. Info from 2024 or earlier is labeled [OLDER].

## 1. Case studies of 2025–26 breakouts

### Takeaway
The 2025 mega-hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights in the Forest) came from teams of about 3 people that built in days to a few months. Each combined a very simple loop (collect, grow or steal, then wait or defend) with a social or griefing twist and regular update-driven event spikes. Growth was then accelerated by publisher or acquirer capital (DoBig/Splitting Point), YouTuber coverage and Roblox's own promotion. By 2026 the copy-and-remix wave ("Steal an Egg", "Grow a Garden 2", brainrot clones) dominates.

### Cited Findings
**Grow a Garden (released 2025; by BMWLux, then Splitting Point/Jandel, with DoBig as a minority holder)**
- Reportedly a solo project by a teenager, built "in like three days". It hit 8.9M concurrent players, while Steam peaked at 11.5M across all games on the same day — [Yahoo/PC Gamer](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/roblox-farming-game-made-teenager-002326583.html); [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- It was drawing about 1,000 CCU under its original creator when Splitting Point took it over. Co-owned by BMWLux and Splitting Point (led by Jandel), with DoBig Studios holding a minority share. About 12 of the studio's 19 devs were moved onto it ("almost all our [dev] staff") — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); [GamesBeat interview with Jandel](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)
- Peak CCU of 22.3M on Aug 23, 2025; DAU peak around 60M; about 30B total plays (Jandel per GamesBeat). Conflicting figure: 21.96M on July 19 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/); contradicted by [Eneba](https://www.eneba.com/hub/news/grow-a-garden-hits-21-9-million-concurrent-players-making-roblox-the-center-of-gaming/). A Guinness record of 21.6M is also cited — [RoStats/thespike summary](https://rostats.io/records)
- Revenue estimate: about $12M and 1.2B hours played in May 2025 (unofficial) — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-grow-a-garden-sees-16-million-ccus/). Jacob Navok (Genvid CEO, opinion on X): "27m peak CCU, $100m+ in revenue in its first two months… made by teams of three people" — [X/@JNavok](https://x.com/JNavok/status/1986966365782036507?lang=en)
- What Jandel credits: updates driven by player feedback, and features built to bring players back — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)
- Live ops: "Admin Abuse" is a recurring event, usually on Saturdays alongside major updates. Jandel uses admin powers to trigger special weather, restock shops and hand out exclusive seeds, pets and packs. It typically starts 30–60 minutes before the update drops, and "Watering Wednesday" runs mid-week. The "Admin Abuse War" (Aug 2025) was a staged, roughly 90-minute feud between Jandel and Sammy (the Steal a Brainrot dev, appearing as a thief-themed NPC), which cross-promoted the two DoBig-linked games — [Grow a Garden Wiki: Admin Abuse](https://growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Admin_Abuse); [Admin Abuse War](https://growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Admin_Abuse_War)
- 2026: "Grow a Garden 2" runs an "Admin Party" that starts 30 minutes before each major update and lasts 30 minutes. The first was Aug 1, 2026 (Fall Harvest update), hosted by Jandel — [GameBoost](https://gameboost.com/blog/grow-a-garden-2-admin-abuse) (third-party guide; I found no other independent coverage of GAG2). Original GAG was still the largest daily-CCU game in May 2026, at about 1.0–1.2M — [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-may-2026/)

**Steal a Brainrot (2025; SpyderSammy, owned by DoBig Studios)**
- Core loop: buy and collect "brainrot" characters (Italian brainrot memes) that generate money, defend your base, and steal other players' brainrots — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- The only Roblox game to pass 25M CCU. RoWatcher puts its peak at 25.8M in Oct 2025 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- Team: "teams of three people" and "$50m+ in revenue in its first month" (Navok's claim, unverified) — [X/@JNavok](https://x.com/JNavok/status/1986966365782036507?lang=en). Navok also estimated $80–90M on Roblox in 2025 — [X/@JNavok](https://x.com/JNavok/status/1990189950017683519). Creator Exchange estimated $11M per month in July 2025 (+117%) with 720M hours played — [X/@CreatorExc](https://x.com/CreatorExc/status/1952868682096574520)
- Decline: from 25.8M peak (Oct 2025) to about 215K CCU in 2026, a roughly 99% drop. Each spike was event-driven and followed by a sharper fall. RoWatcher credits its staying power to weekly content drops rather than the meme — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- Current revenue estimates vary widely: $1.4M/month ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/7709344486/steal-a-brainrot)), $2.0–3.5M/month ([profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/steal-a-brainrot)), and $577.5M lifetime ([bloxprices](https://www.bloxprices.com/stats/steal-a-brainrot/)). All are tracker estimates and conflict with each other.
- Fortnite copied the mode ("Steal the Brainrot") and peaked above 540K CCU — [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/fortnite-steal-the-brainrot-internet-culture,1532127). There are also reports of a legal dispute — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-game-steal-a-brainrot-sues-fortnite/) (details not verified)

**99 Nights in the Forest (released June 2025; trio Cracky4, ForyxeV, Viridial)**
- Planned as a one-week project that grew into three months. Built March to June 2025, their fastest development ever (previous projects took about 6 months). Peak CCU of 14M — [DevForum Creator Spotlight](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940)
- Concept: the "survive N days" trend, plus inspiration from Dead Rails, plus a cozy forest theme. Short-session co-op survival horror. Tech note: they push as much logic to the client as possible for responsiveness — [DevForum Creator Spotlight](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940)

**Dead Rails (created Jan 1, 2025; RCM Games)**
- RCM Games is a prolific group with about 70 titles, and Dead Rails far outperforms the rest — [RoMonitor](https://romonitorstats.com/experience/116495829188952/); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/7018190066/dead-rails-endless)
- Roblox's official "Game Changers" profile of developer Evan: he spent months chasing the "perfect" game, then leaned into his own goofy voice (history, trains, buff zombie-werewolves) — [NWPS media (Roblox Game Changers)](https://media.nwps.fi/2026/05/28/game-changers-evan-and-the-goofy-vision-behind-dead-rails/)

- Peak CCU: 1,476,198 per Rolimons — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/116495829188952); GameAnalytics instead cites over 200K at peak with a weekend spike above 600K (date unclear) — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/dead-rails-and-the-hit-makers-formula)

### Inferences
- Repeatable recipe: (1) a trending noun or meme (garden, brainrot, eggs, forest), (2) an idle or collect loop that pays out while offline or AFK, (3) a social conflict mechanic (stealing, trading, co-op survival), (4) scarcity through timed shop restocks, (5) a weekly update plus a live admin event as a "be online at X time" appointment.
- Several hits started at small scale and were scaled up by an operator with staff and capital (GAG: 1K CCU before Splitting Point). This suggests a viable small-team path: build lots of cheap prototypes, then partner with or sell to a live-ops operator once one gets traction.
- Peaks are event-driven and decay fast, so revenue is front-loaded into the first weeks.

### Gaps
- No verified team size or build time for Steal a Brainrot (only Navok's "three people"), Dead Rails, Forsaken or Blue Lock Rivals.
- No official revenue figures for any game. All are tracker estimates that conflict, sometimes by 10x.
- No reliable source for Grow a Garden 2's launch date or performance beyond one guide site.
- No reliable sourcing found for the claim that KreekCraft "earned $1M from Steal a Brainrot".

## 2. Trend patterns (genres, lifespans, age split, YouTube/TikTok)

### Takeaway
The fastest-growing categories are steal-and-defend tycoons, idle grow and collect simulators, short-session co-op horror or survival, anime fighting and sports, and fashion or social games. Meme content and clones spread within days, while individual clones can lose a large share of players within weeks. In 2026 Roblox explicitly splits discovery by age (shorter, fast-turnover games for younger players; deeper retention for older ones), and 18+ DAU is the fastest-growing cohort.

### Cited Findings
- Fastest-growing categories: steal-and-defend tycoons, idle-grow simulators, co-op horror, anime fighters, fashion social games, all built on a small set of mechanics — [Game-Ace](https://game-ace.com/blog/roblox-trends-in-gaming/)
- The brainrot trend spawned copycats such as Plants vs Brainrots, Brainrot Evolution and Brainrot Training, because the collect-and-generate loop is easy to vary — [ABNewswire/FinancialContent](https://markets.financialcontent.com/clarkebroadcasting.mymotherlode/article/abnewswire-2025-11-7-how-roblox-brainrot-games-are-taking-over)
- Lifespan data: Steal a Brainrot fell about 99% from peak. Swing Obby for Brainrots dropped from 87K to 57K CCU in its first five weeks — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- New imitations appear every week — [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/copycat-roblox-games)
- Steal an Egg spawned a Clone to Steal Eggs remix within weeks of its peak (Sept 2026) — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/76943966208523)
- Hit titles share "virtually nothing in common beyond short and snappy titles" (an argument that trends are hard to predict) — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/roblox-steal-brainrot-fad-explanation/)
- Naavik: the 2025 hits drove Roblox DAU and bookings up 52% and 55% YoY, and "in network effect-driven industries, the winners tend to keep on winning" — [Naavik, State of UGC Games 2026](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/) (excerpt only)
- Age split (official, Q2 2026): DAU of 123M (+10%), 18+ DAU +32% YoY. Among age-checked users, 38% were 13–17 as of June 30, 2026. Bookings slowed (+8%) because per-hour monetization fell, "most notably with younger cohorts in the U.S. and Canada", and engagement shifted toward lower-monetizing games — [Roblox Q2 2026 shareholder letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm); [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q2-2026-slides-revenue-rises-36-as-bookings-growth-stalls-93CH-4826381)
- RDC 2026 (Sept 2026): ranking is now age-aware. Younger players are shown shorter, quick-turnover games and older players are shown games with retention depth, measured over 28 days. "Moments" (a video-first discovery tab) is fully live in the US — [Panstag (blog summary of RDC 2026)](https://www.panstag.com/2026/09/rdc-solo-creator-guide.html) (secondary; not confirmed in an official source)
- April 2026: Roblox is pushing high-fidelity games for 18+ players and raised the qualifying DevEx rate by 42% for spend by US 18+ players in eligible games, from June 8 — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42)
- YouTube and TikTok: Dead Rails spiked after Roblox's official promotion, then big YouTubers amplified it — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/dead-rails-and-the-hit-makers-formula). One marketing blog claims one in five gaming TikToks mentions Roblox (mid-2025) — [InReels](https://www.inreels.ai/blog/what-are-roblox-videos) (unverified). Creators were given rare "OG" brainrots during events — [fan-site claim via search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot) (unverified). KreekCraft launched his own "brainrot" game to fund his studio's larger project, with about $100K spent on legal and early hires — [Tubefilter newsletter](https://newsletter.tubefilter.com/p/what-s-the-roi-for-roblox-devs)
- DevForum advice: run Roblox ads first, and if they perform, approach TikTok and YouTube creators — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-has-our-game-failed/2921427)

### Inferences
- For a small team, trend-chasing speed matters most in the first 1–3 weeks after a new meme or loop appears. Ship a remix (theme swap plus one new twist) inside that window, and don't expect a long tail unless you can run weekly updates.
- With 2026 age-aware ranking, an under-13-friendly short-loop game and a 16+/18+ deeper-retention game are effectively different products with different discovery paths and eligibility rules (see section 6).

### Gaps
- No hard data on what under-13 versus 13+ players play by genre. Only cohort-level monetization and DAU figures are public.
- No quantitative study of how much YouTuber or TikTok coverage drives CCU for a given game.
- Cozy and dress-up trend data for 2026 is thin.

## 3. Process: prototype → test → launch → live ops

### Takeaway
There is no official "kill criteria". Community and benchmark data points to roughly 10% D1 retention and about 8–10+ minutes average session as the minimum signal worth investing in. GameAnalytics treats games with 19+ minute sessions, double-digit D1 and about 2 sessions per day as "hits". Hits are built fast (3 days to 4 months), launched small, then grown with weekly updates and scheduled live events. Since June 2026 Roblox's algorithm rewards 28-day retention, which shifts the weight from launch spikes to sustained return play.

### Cited Findings
- Build times reported: GAG about 3 days ([Yahoo/PC Gamer](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/roblox-farming-game-made-teenager-002326583.html)); 99 Nights about 3 months, planned as one week ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940)); Fisch about 4 months solo ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-woozynate-makes-a-splash-with-fisch/3269481)); RCM Games about 70 titles before and around Dead Rails ([RoMonitor](https://romonitorstats.com/experience/116495829188952/))
- GameAnalytics 2025 Roblox benchmarks: 0–6 minute session games are "fragile" (D1 under 10%, about one session per day, negligible ARPPU). 7–18 minute games hold attention but not frequency. 19+ minute games are "hits" (double-digit D1, about 2 sessions per day) — [GameAnalytics 2025 report](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2025-roblox-report)
- GameAnalytics 2026 benchmarks (games with 1M+ MAU only): median D1 10.3%, D7 1.6%, D30 0.5%; median session 9.8 minutes — [GameAnalytics 2026 report](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)
- DevForum rule of thumb: aim for at least 8 minutes playtime and 10% D1. Struggling games commonly report D1 of 2.3–2.5% and 3–5.5 minutes playtime — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/suggestions-to-improve-average-playtime-d1-retention/3883895); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-improve-average-playtime-and-day-1-retention/4565940)
- Official retention definitions (D1, D7 and D30 by first-play cohort) — [Roblox Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)
- Sponsored ads caveat: one developer reports ~3% D1 with ads on versus 5–8% with ads off, and says ad traffic degrades the metrics discovery uses (bug report, not confirmed by Roblox) — [DevForum bug report](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351). Others say ads are mainly useful for initial visibility when home recommendations aren't sending traffic — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/does-sponsoring-improve-retention/4073864)
- Discovery algorithm: on June 15, 2026 Recommended For You moved from a 7-day to a 28-day window that measures long-term retention, and Roblox published its full list of signals with weights — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042). "Today's Picks" is becoming "Standout Games" for novel games, with a creator nomination form — [DevForum: How we are improving Home](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571). [OLDER, Mar 2025] An earlier RFY revamp added creator analytics — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441)
- Roblox said the algorithm changes hurt bookings in Q2 2026 (near-term impact on younger cohorts was "larger than we anticipated") — [Roblox Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)
- Live ops patterns: Saturday major updates plus an Admin Abuse event, and a mid-week mini event ("Watering Wednesday") — [GAG Wiki](https://growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Admin_Abuse). Weekly content drops sustain Steal a Brainrot — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals). Limited-time modes with event shops in Blue Lock Rivals — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/blue-lock-rivals-summer-patch-notes-may-17-2025). Halloween updates drive all-time peaks (Forsaken peaked Nov 1, 2025; DTI 1.1M on Halloween day) — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/18687417158); [The Mary Sue](https://www.themarysue.com/gigi-creator-of-roblox-sensation-dress-to-impress-is-embroiled-in-a-massive-controversy/). Cross-game crossover events (Jandel vs. Sammy war) — [GAG Wiki](https://growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Admin_Abuse_War)
- Onboarding: let players jump in and have fun immediately. A long tutorial is a red flag. Most players are on low-end devices, so performance matters — [DevForum: Why Games Fail](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-games-fail-and-how-to-fix-it-a-comprehensive-tutorial/1588819); [DevForum: So your game died](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/so-your-game-died-a-thread-on-game-design-and-the-market/2644450)
- Scope: adding features before the core design is finished "exponentially increases the execution burden" — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/plugin-why-you-never-finish-roblox-games/4223242)

### Inferences
- A pipeline a small team can copy:
  1. Spend 3–10 days building a vertical slice of a trend remix.
  2. Run a small ad test or a friends/Discord playtest for about 1 week.
  3. Read D1, average session and payer conversion.
  4. Decide whether to continue or kill.
  5. If continuing, commit to a weekly update plus a weekend live event for 4–8 weeks.
- Suggested (inferred, not official) kill thresholds after 7–14 days and at least a few thousand visits:
  - D1 under about 5% **and** session under about 5 minutes: kill or pivot.
  - D1 at 5–10% **or** session at 5–8 minutes: iterate one or two cycles on onboarding and the core loop.
  - D1 at or above about 10% **and** session at or above 8–10 minutes: scale with ads, creators and updates. Watch D7 because of the 28-day RFY window.
- Ad-traffic metrics may understate organic quality, so compare ad cohorts with organic and friend cohorts before killing a game.

### Gaps
- No official Roblox soft-launch framework or kill thresholds exist. The thresholds above are inferred from community and benchmark data.
- I could not access RDC 2025 talk content directly.
- Discord and community management practices of top studios (group sizes, mod staffing) were not found in sources I could reach.

## 4. Team and business: structures, rev-share, freelancers, publishers and funds, IP/clone risk

### Takeaway
Hits come from 1–3 person cores. Scaling often happens through acquisition or co-ownership with operators like DoBig, Splitting Point and Voldex, which buy rising games and staff them (GAG: about 12 devs). Freelancers cost about $15–90/hr depending on role. Funding options include Roblox's Creator Fund ([OLDER, 2024]: $500K-minimum grants per project) and third-party funds (Gamefam Creator Fund at $1K–$100K+, 18+ only). Copying is endemic and largely unenforced, and even Fortnite copied a Roblox mode.

### Cited Findings
- Navok (opinion): recent hits were made by teams of three — [X/@JNavok](https://x.com/JNavok/status/1986966365782036507?lang=en)

## 5. Common reasons new Roblox games fail

### Takeaway
Most failures come from weak first-session retention (slow onboarding, unclear loop, poor performance on low-end devices) and the cold-start problem: no initial players means no algorithmic promotion. Scope creep and over-polishing before validating the loop come next. The 2026 algorithm makes D1, D7 and long-term return play even more decisive.

### Cited Findings
- Players leave when the value of continuing falls below other games. Fixes are unique content plus social or randomized elements — [DevForum: Why Games Fail](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-games-fail-and-how-to-fix-it-a-comprehensive-tutorial/1588819)
- "Fun is essential but isn't the only factor". Market and timing matter too — [DevForum: So your game died](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/so-your-game-died-a-thread-on-game-design-and-the-market/2644450)
- Roblox won't promote a game without an initial playerbase, so paid ads or social traffic are needed at first — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-has-our-game-failed/2921427)
- Success is tied to session length, retention and monetization efficiency, not technical quality — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-your-technically-superior-game-isn%E2%80%99t-succeeding-on-roblox-and-why-the-algorithm-ignores-it/4461606)
- Long tutorials and intros kill retention. Low-end device performance matters — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-games-fail-and-how-to-fix-it-a-comprehensive-tutorial/1588819)
- Overambition and adding features before the core is done — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/plugin-why-you-never-finish-roblox-games/4223242)
- The Dead Rails dev spent months chasing a "perfect" game before finding a distinctive voice — [NWPS / Roblox Game Changers](https://media.nwps.fi/2026/05/28/game-changers-evan-and-the-goofy-vision-behind-dead-rails/)
- Sudden drops after updates (regressions) — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sudden-drop-on-playtime-and-retention-with-update/3880064)
- Ad traffic may depress the metrics used for organic ranking (single developer's claim) — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351)

### Inferences
- New failure modes in 2025–26: publishing without meeting eligibility (ID or age check, maturity questionnaire), or using now-restricted mechanics (rewarded media feeds) that remove a game from Kids/Select.
- Building one large game instead of many small tests lowers the chance of a hit. RCM Games' roughly 70 titles before Dead Rails illustrates the volume approach.

### Gaps
- No systematic data-backed postmortem dataset. These are community opinions.

## 6. Roblox platform changes in 2025–26 affecting small creators

### Takeaway
Since Dec 2025, publishing a public game requires ID verification or a 2025+ purchase, plus a completed maturity questionnaire. In 2026 publishing moved to audience tiers: reaching under-16 audiences via "Roblox Kids and Select" requires a verified creator, 2FA, possibly Plus/Premium, and an engagement evaluation. Chat requires age checks worldwide from Jan 2026. Creator Rewards (July 2025) pays for engaged Active Spenders and new-user spend. The discovery algorithm moved to a 28-day retention view in June 2026, and reward-driven media feeds were restricted in Aug 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Publishing eligibility (Dec 2025):** to publish or update public experiences, creators must be ID-verified **or** have made a real-money or gift-card purchase since Jan 1, 2025. Experiences need a content maturity label via the Content & Maturity questionnaire. Roblox said most creators were already eligible — [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-requirements-to-publish-and-update-public-experiences/4143953)
- **2026 audience tiers:** "Personal Use" is open to any account in good standing. The 16+ tier requires an age check (ID or facial estimation), good standing and one question about age-restricted content — [Roblox Help: How to Publish](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313890-How-to-Publish-Games-on-Roblox). "Roblox Kids and Select" (reaching under-16 players) requires a verified creator account (facial estimation if under 18, government ID if 18+), 2-Step Verification, and an evaluation needing 250 unique plays from highly engaged players in the last 60 days — [Roblox Creator Docs: Kids and Select](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select). Third-party guides add an active Roblox Plus subscription or Premium for at least 2 months, and a 500 engaged-player figure (conflicts with the official 250) — [BloxBot](https://bloxbot.ai/guide/roblox-new-publishing-requirements-2026); [RoGuides](https://www.roguides.com/blog/roblox-new-publishing-requirements-2026-are-you-eligible). The Creator Updates page notes the Highly Engaged Player requirement was halved from 500 to 250 — [Roblox Creator Updates](https://create.roblox.com/updates)
- **Age checks and chat (Jan 2026, worldwide):** chat requires an age check by facial estimation (via vendor Persona, images deleted after processing) or ID, or parental verification. Six age bands: under 9, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18–20, 21+. Chat is limited to the same or adjacent bands — [Roblox IR](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2026/Roblox-Requires-Users-Worldwide-to-Age-Check-to-Access-Chat/default.aspx); [Roblox Help](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/39143693116052-Understanding-Age-Checks-on-Roblox); [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/roblox-rolls-out-facial-age-estimation-for-chat-access-globally). Team Create collaboration is limited to age-checked users of similar ages and trusted friends (from early 2026) — [Roblox IR](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2026/Roblox-Requires-Users-Worldwide-to-Age-Check-to-Access-Chat/default.aspx). Age-check penetration reached 57% globally, about 70% in the US and UK, by Q2 2026 — [Roblox Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm). A claim that full ID has been required for social features since May 19, 2026 could not be verified — [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/roblox-age-verification-2026/)
- **Creator Rewards (launched July 2025; replaced Engagement-Based Payouts and Creator Affiliates):**
  - Daily Engagement Rewards: 5 Robux when an Active Spender plays at least 10 minutes in one of the first three experiences they launch that day.
  - Audience Expansion: 35% of the first $100 in Robux purchases made by new or returning users (60+ days inactive) during their first two months.
  - Payouts have a 60-day hold.
  - Sources: [DevForum: Introducing Creator Rewards](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628); [DevForum: Creator Rewards is Live](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-rewards-is-live/3838257). Roblox noted the rates may change. Creator Reward analytics were added later — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-new-creator-reward-analytics-better-data-better-decisions/4146591)
- **DevEx (June 8, 2026):** +42% on the qualifying DevEx rate for spend by US 18+ players in eligible games. A third party describes this as 26.6% to 37.8% for R15 games — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42); [Spaceport](https://www.spaceport.xyz/blog/roblox-developer-rewards-changes)
- **Discovery (June 15, 2026):** RFY moved to a 28-day long-term retention view, with published signals. The "Standout Games" curated sort has a nomination form — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571)
- **Reward-driven media feeds (Aug 25, 2026):** games combining a short-video, image or story feed **plus** autoplay or infinite scroll **plus** rewards for continued watching are removed from Kids and Select until redesigned. Rewarded video ads that players opt into are still allowed — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188); [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25146/roblox-blocks-rewarded-infinite-scroll-on-kids-accounts). Steal an Egg was pulled and then relisted without the feature — [Slashdot](https://games.slashdot.org/story/26/08/28/0027222/roblox-is-cracking-down-on-games-that-reward-kids-for-doomscrolling)
- **RDC 2026 (Sept 2026):** age-aware ranking; Moments video tab live in the US; launching games from Chrome via the Experience Details Page without installing the app is planned by end of 2026; Roblox Wallet rollout for eligible creators is on the late-2026 roadmap — [Panstag](https://www.panstag.com/2026/09/rdc-solo-creator-guide.html) (secondary); [Roblox Roadmap](https://create.roblox.com/roadmap)
- Market context: Q2 2026 bookings +8% (slowdown). Q3 guidance was weaker, citing algorithm and age-check changes. Shares fell about 14% after hours — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q2-2026-slides-revenue-rises-36-as-bookings-growth-stalls-93CH-4826381); [InsiderFinance](https://www.insiderfinance.io/news/roblox-q2-earnings-weigh-on-guidance-after-safety-changes). Q3 earnings were due Oct 29, 2026 — [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/roblox-stock-is-down-37-in-2026-heres-what-its-new-viral-hit-has-to-prove-on-october-29)

### Inferences
- Small-team checklist before launch:
  - Every publishing account is age-checked or ID-verified, with 2FA on.
  - The maturity questionnaire is done.
  - Decide whether the target is under-16 (Kids/Select: needs a 250-engaged-player evaluation, so plan a 16+ or limited audience phase first) or 16+/18+ (higher DevEx rate in eligible games, deeper retention favored).
  - Avoid rewarded media feeds.
- Creator Rewards favors games that are among a payer's first three launches each day and keep them 10+ minutes. Daily-habit design (restock timers, daily rewards) lines up with both the payouts and the 28-day ranking.

- No official confirmation of RDC 2026 details beyond secondary blog summaries.
